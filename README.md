# My Favorite Movie: Fast & Furious 9 🔥

![Fast & Furious 9](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0c/F9_film_poster.jpg)

## 🎬 Overview
*Fast & Furious 9* (also known as F9) is a 2021 action film directed by *Justin Lin*.  
It continues the story of *Dominic Toretto* and his crew as they face new threats — including Dom’s long-lost brother, *Jakob Toretto*.

## 💥 Why I Like It
- The car stunts and action scenes are exciting and over the top.  
- It shows the importance of *family* and loyalty.  
- The soundtrack and visuals are top-tier.  

## 🚦 Main Characters
1. Dominic Toretto (Vin Diesel)  
2. Letty Ortiz (Michelle Rodriguez)  
3. Roman Pearce (Tyrese Gibson)  
4. Tej Parker (Ludacris)  
5. Mia Toretto (Jordana Brewster)  
6. Jakob Toretto (John Cena)

*“I don’t have friends. I got family.”* – **Dominic Toretto**
